Last Updated: August 2026

Exotic Dairy EAD ("Exotic Dairy", "we", "our" or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy and handling personal information in a transparent, secure and responsible manner. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it and the rights available to you.

When you contact us through this website, we may collect information such as:

Personal information is processed exclusively for legitimate business purposes, including:

Legal Basis

Where applicable, we process personal information based on our legitimate interest in responding to business enquiries, fulfilling legal obligations or, where required, your consent in accordance with applicable data protection laws, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Data Sharing

Information may be shared only with trusted service providers supporting the operation, security or hosting of this website, or where disclosure is required by applicable law.

International Data Transfers

Where personal information is processed by trusted service providers located outside your country of residence, Exotic Dairy EAD will take appropriate measures to ensure that such processing complies with applicable data protection laws.

Data Retention

Personal information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your enquiry, maintain legitimate business records or comply with applicable legal requirements.

Data Security

We implement appropriate administrative, technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, loss or destruction.
