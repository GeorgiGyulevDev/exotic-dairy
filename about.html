Lyudmil Shopov

Animal Breeding & Livestock Development Specialist

Lyudmil focuses on the biological and technological foundation of the Exotic Dairy livestock ecosystem — healthy animals, responsible breeding, appropriate nutrition and the sustainable development of camel and donkey populations.

His work covers herd management, breeding and reproduction, animal feeding and nutrition, husbandry technologies, animal identification, health monitoring and the practical development of responsible livestock production systems.

Animal welfare and humane treatment are central to his work. By combining proper nutrition, breeding, husbandry technology and care in harmony with nature, he helps create the conditions for healthy animals, consistent milk quality and responsible long-term production.

Bernard Faye is a veterinarian and internationally recognized camel specialist whose expertise supports Exotic Dairy in the scientific and practical development of its camel-related activities.
