How It Works

How the partnership works.

More than a financial contribution — a long-term partnership designed to connect businesses, expertise and opportunities within the Exotic Dairy ecosystem.

1

Join

Your Founder Contribution establishes your participation in the Exotic Dairy ecosystem.

2

Participate

While the partnership is active, you participate according to the terms defined in your Founder Partnership agreement.

3

Grow Together

Your business and Exotic Dairy actively seek opportunities to strengthen each other through supply, distribution, research, product development and market access.

4

Stay & Earn — or Exit

Stay in the ecosystem and continue participating according to the partnership terms, or after the minimum five-year partnership period choose to request an exit according to the applicable terms.

Some strategic partnership agreements may include predefined exit options under applicable terms.
