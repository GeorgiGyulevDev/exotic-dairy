Research Center

Where Research Becomes Reality.

At the heart of the Experience Hub will be a research and innovation environment focused on understanding the unique properties of camel and donkey milk.

The center is designed to connect scientific knowledge with practical product development, creating a physical platform where researchers, producers and technology partners can work toward new functional products and applications.

Rare milk research Product development Testing and validation Scientific collaboration

Animal Experience

Meet the Animals Behind the Story.

The Experience Hub will bring visitors closer to camels and donkeys while keeping responsible breeding, professional care and animal welfare at the center of every interaction.

Animals will live in controlled and professionally managed environments designed around their welfare rather than unrestricted visitor interaction.

Guided encounters Responsible breeding Animal welfare Education and awareness

From Milk to Product

Follow the Journey.

One of the central ideas behind the Experience Hub is to make the complete Exotic Dairy journey visible and understandable.

Visitors will be able to see how responsible animal breeding connects with research, how research becomes product development, and how those products eventually reach the consumer.

Animals and raw milk Scientific research Product development Production and experience

Showroom & Tasting

Taste the Innovation.

The Hub will include a dedicated showroom and tasting environment where visitors can discover the products emerging from the Exotic Dairy research and development ecosystem.

The space can also become a platform for prototypes, future product launches, educational tastings and direct feedback from visitors.
